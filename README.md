# SportsEvidence — Scientific Publications to Datasets

> Transforming sports science PDFs into structured, 
> machine-readable datasets to address the 
> low-participant-number problem in sports research.

## The Problem

Most sports science studies are never accessible 
as raw data. Results are buried in PDFs, 
sample sizes are small (median n=19 in top journals), 
and findings cannot be systematically compared.
This makes meta-analyses slow, manual, and rare.

## What This Does

This tool extracts structured data from sports 
science publications automatically:

PDF / DOI → structured CSV dataset

Each row = one study. Each column = one variable
(sample size, effect size, population, 
measurement method, confidence interval, p-values ...).

## Current Scope

Focused on: Sports Performance

## Long-Term Vision

A living, open-source dataset that grows as 
new research is published - allowing researchers 
with small samples to benchmark their findings 
against accumulated evidence.
