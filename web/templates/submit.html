Submit a Study

Contribute a sports science intervention study to the open dataset.

Full-text PDF of the paper. Max 32 MB.

What we accept

  • Randomised controlled trials (RCT)
  • Non-randomised controlled trials
  • Crossover designs
  • Pre–post intervention studies
  • Any sport or exercise modality

What we don't accept

  • Systematic reviews / meta-analyses
  • Observational / epidemiological studies
  • Case reports / case series
  • Non-sports clinical populations only
  • Conference abstracts

Review process

Every submission is manually reviewed before extraction. We check that the study design qualifies and the PDF contains usable numerical data. Typical review time: 1–3 days.
